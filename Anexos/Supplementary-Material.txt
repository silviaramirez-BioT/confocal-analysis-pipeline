APPENDIX A. Computational Image Analysis Protocol

A.1. Algorithm Description (Fiji/ImageJ)
To ensure reproducibility and minimize manual selection bias, a semi-automated 
segmentation routine was developed. The workflow operates on the following logic:

1. Detection Strategy ("Merge Mask"): 
   Unlike traditional nuclear segmentation, this algorithm generates an additive 
   mask by summing intensities from all channels (Sum = Blue + Green + Red). 
   This ensures the detection of cells with weak nuclear staining but high 
   parietal marker expression.

2. Pre-processing:
   - Background Subtraction: Rolling Ball algorithm (radius=50 px) to remove 
     diffuse noise.
   - Smoothing: Gaussian Blur filter (sigma=2) to homogenize boundaries.

3. Segmentation: 
   The "Triangle" thresholding algorithm is applied, optimized to maximize the 
   detection of weak signals in histograms with long tails. Subsequently, a 
   Watershed algorithm separates adjacent cells.

4. Execution Modes:
   - Automatic: High-speed batch processing.
   - Curated (Editable): Includes programmed pauses for human validation 
     (artifact removal or manual ROI addition).

A.2. Data Dictionary and Biological Interpretation
The post-processing software (Python) generates the following metrics for 
each individual ROI:

a) Signal/Nucleus Ratio (Variable: Ratio_Green_Blue)
   - Technical Definition: Mean Marker Intensity / Mean Nucleus Intensity.
   - Biological Interpretation: Relative Abundance. It normalizes the signal 
     against DNA content, correcting for technical variations (focus, laser 
     power). This is the primary comparative metric.

b) Physical Coverage (Variable: Perc_Area %)
   - Technical Definition: Percentage of total cell area exceeding the 
     fluorescence threshold.
   - Biological Interpretation: Spatial Distribution. 
     (Low % = Punctate/vesicular signal; High % = Uniform distribution).

c) Integrated Density (Variable: IntDen)
   - Technical Definition: Mean Intensity x Total Cell Area.
   - Biological Interpretation: Total Load. Represents the absolute amount 
     of accumulated substance. Useful when cell size varies significantly 
     between groups.

d) Absolute Intensity %
   - Technical Definition: Intensity scaled (0-100%) according to bit-depth 
     (8/12/16-bit).
   - Biological Interpretation: Sensor saturation indicator. Useful for 
     acquisition quality control.

A.3. Statistical Treatment
Due to the heterogeneous nature of biological samples (e.g., focal vesicle 
accumulation), fluorescence data do not follow a normal distribution 
(Shapiro-Wilk test, p < 0.05). Therefore, this study reports the Median 
and Interquartile Range (IQR) as robust measures of central tendency and 
dispersion, neutralizing bias from outliers.
